**Classification ambiguity:** The scalar's construction is unspecified; the compared document roles are clear.

**Mechanical evidence recheck:** All four source locations and printed statements were visually confirmed. The rechecker reproduced the definition-to-scalar gap, retained S_W/statistical_results and distinguished direct printed facts from possible production explanations.
