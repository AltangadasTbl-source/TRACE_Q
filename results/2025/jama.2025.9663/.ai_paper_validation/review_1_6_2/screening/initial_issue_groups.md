No Wilcoxon counterpart is reported in the supplied REPORT map/raw text.

**Rule and B1/B2/B3 rationale:** B1: an explicitly different planned-versus-reported analysis specification is within D, and report-versus-plan model specification is an eligible comparison irrespective of whether every stay result is key secondary. B2: a survival-stratified rank-sum contrast and an adjusted competing-risk regression are distinct procedures; the final protocol retains the former while the final SAP describes the latter, with no statement saying whether replacement or addition is intended. B3: this changes the prespecified analysis attached to the named ICU/hospital sHRs and P values, including whether those results replace the protocol test. The report clearly identifies its implemented method; that clarity does not identify the status of the different retained prospective method. No arithmetic/display-precision calculation or test-significance conflict is asserted.

**Alternatives and classification ambiguity:** A detailed later SAP may intentionally replace a shorter protocol analysis description; p70 says analyses will be lodged in a SAP, and p136 documents the later SAP update. Alternatively both analyses may have been retained. The difference also persists between final supplied versions, so an obsolete original protocol alone is not the basis. No preferred method or claim about the suitability of Fine–Gray is proposed.

**Human question:** Did the final SAP replace the protocol Wilcoxon stay comparisons, or were both retained, and where is that relationship and any retained result documented?

## Group: Pilot adherence numerator and progression direction

**Finding provenance:** F-NUM-001, INCLUDE. **Qualifying route:** Route B. **Focus:** D, planned decision rule; C, quantity definition. **Proposed domain:** plan_material. **Primary relationship and all relationships:** P_P. REPORT adherence information is corroboration only and is not a conflicting relationship.

**All affected locations:** PLAN original SAP [p95, Table 1 and footnote](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=95); PLAN original SAP [p98, §3.2.2 continuation](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=98); PLAN final SAP [p118, Table 1 and footnote](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=118); PLAN final protocol [p69, Table 2 footnote](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=69). Decision consequences: PLAN final protocol [p70, §4.2.1](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=70), original SAP [p96, Table 1 text continuation](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=96), and final SAP [p119, Table 1 text continuation](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=119).

**Independently observed facts:** Both SAP images label the row Treatment adherence and print Green ≥75% cases, Amber 50–75% cases, Red <50% cases. The footnote explicitly says, for progression criteria, that this percentage is calculated from conservative-group patients with one or more adjudicated protocol deviations. Original SAP p98 defines patient-level adherence as not having experienced any protocol deviation. Final protocol p69 defines adherence as adjustments to ventilator settings or supplemental oxygen as per protocol. Final protocol p70 states all-green progression unchanged, any-amber amendment, any-red stop, with final funder decision following TSC recommendation. The final SAP retains the deviation numerator footnote.

**Rule and calculation:** For one matched conservative population, let d be the fraction with one or more adjudicated deviations and a the fraction with none; a=1−d. Green applied to d≥.75 corresponds to a≤.25, whereas Green applied to adherence a≥.75 corresponds to d≤.25. Red applied to d<.50 corresponds to a>.50. Illustrative a=.80 and d=.20 yields Green with the adherence numerator and Red with the printed deviation numerator; these are algebraic examples, not observed trial values. The conflict is in event membership and direction of a decision metric, not a rounded observation, so half-unit numerical precision intervals do not settle it.

**B1/B2/B3 rationale:** B1: planned progression rules are specifically within D and are not peripheral plan background. B2: the explicit footnote defines the quantity used for progression as deviations, while the explicit adherence definition and protocol use adherence; both are mathematically distinct quantities. A reader can infer a likely intended complement, but the package does not identify which numerator was applied to the thresholds. This exceeds a field-resolvable shorthand. B3: the complementary numerators can reverse the named pilot traffic-light decision and alter the extracted progression metric. No actual pilot color or full-trial conclusion is inferred.

**Alternatives:** The deviation fraction may have been intended as a separately reported nonadherence statistic while the threshold actually used adherence. Alternatively the footnote may define the applied statistic, leaving its direction to be clarified. No amendment in the supplied final definition identifies an overriding numerator. Adjacent equality conventions at exact threshold boundaries are distinct and are not included in this issue.

**Human question:** Was the progression fraction based on no adjudicated deviation or at least one deviation, and which numerator was used for the color thresholds?

## Group: Hospital missing-duration censoring at one hour versus one day

**Finding provenance:** F-XS-002 and F-ST1-001, both INCLUDE. **Qualifying route:** Route B. **Focus:** C. **Proposed domain:** statistical_results. **Primary relationship and all relationships:** S_C. The SAP/report choice of censoring versus MICE belongs to the preceding group, rather than being duplicated here.

**All affected locations:** REPORT main [p8, Table 2 hospital-duration sHR and footnote b](../../../jama_martin_2025_oi_250042_1753377747.91025.pdf#page=8); REPORT results supplement [p24, eTable 7 hospital-duration row and footnote e](../../../joi250042supp2_prod_1753377747.93025.pdf#page=24).

**Independently observed facts:** Main footnote b explicitly names both ICU and acute-hospital duration and states that missing discharge times were included as censored 1 hour after randomization. Supplement footnote e explicitly assigns 1 hour for ICU and 1 day for acute-hospital duration. It lists 1677 (10.2%) missing hospital durations. Main Table 2 prints hospital adjusted sHR 0.98 (95% CI 0.94 to 1.02), P=.27, in both available-case and imputed columns.

**Calculation and precision:** One day=24 hours, giving exact operational times 1 h versus 24 h and a 23 h difference. Conservatively allowing half-unit rounding of the printed integers gives main [0.5,1.5] h and supplement [0.5,1.5] day=[12,36] h; minimum gap=10.5 h and relative discrepancy against the printed main value=10.5/1=10.5>.05. The specification discrepancy remains even under those unusually broad display intervals.

**B1/B2/B3 rationale:** B1: contradictory explicit missing-data censoring times are statistical method logic, not a peripheral per-time-point contributor count. B2: both footnotes name the same hospital outcome; the supplemental distinction between ICU hour-scale and hospital day-scale does not identify whether the main or supplemental hospital time generated the analysis. Calendar-day measurement alone does not make 1 hour equal 1 day. B3: the unresolved time is attached to the named hospital sHR 0.98 analysis and affects the specification a data extractor would take. No magnitude/significance change to that sHR is inferred.

**Alternatives:** The main may have generalized its ICU time to hospital stay, or the supplemental hospital time may differ from implementation. Neither states it describes a different analysis. The identical displayed sHRs cannot identify the implemented cutoff. This is an explicit quantity-changing definition, rather than an isolated missing unit whose intended reading is obvious from values.

**Human question:** Were the missing acute-hospital discharge durations censored at 1 hour or 1 day after randomization in the reported model?
