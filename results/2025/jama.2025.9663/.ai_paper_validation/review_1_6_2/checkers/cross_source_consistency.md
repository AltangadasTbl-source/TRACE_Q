### F-XS-001 — Planned stratification-only primary-outcome analysis has no reported counterpart

- **Compared locations and roles:** PLAN [final protocol v1.8 p70, §4.2.2](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=70), with the same requirement in PLAN [original protocol p30](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=30); REPORT [main p3, Statistical Analysis](../../../jama_martin_2025_oi_250042_1753377747.91025.pdf#page=3), [p6, Primary and Secondary Outcomes](../../../jama_martin_2025_oi_250042_1753377747.91025.pdf#page=6) and [p8, Table2/footnotea](../../../jama_martin_2025_oi_250042_1753377747.91025.pdf#page=8). All other supplied REPORT pages were searched for the missing counterpart.
- **Printed statements:** The final protocol says primary90d mortality will be analyzed both adjusted ONLY for site,HIE,brain pathology excludingHIE,sepsis and adjusted for additional baseline covariates. The main reports fully adjusted RD0.7 (−0.7,2.0), P.28 and an **unadjusted** RD0.5 (−1.0,2.0). Table2's available-case and multiply-imputed columns both use the full covariates in footnotea; neither is labeled stratification-only.
- **Comparison logic:** Unadjusted ≠ adjusted for site/diagnostic strata. Available-case ≠ adjustment-set restriction. None of these reported columns identifies the planned stratification-only primary-outcome regression. PLAN [final SAPpp126–127](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=126) details the fully adjusted primary model, but does not explicitly cancel the retained final-protocol request.
- **Focus area:** D. **Proposed review domain:** `protocol_report`. **Relationship type:** `P_R`. **All relationships:** `P_R`.
- **Qualifying route:** Route A, category5.
- **Affected result or extracted value:** The planned additional90d mortality estimate adjusted only for randomization strata, including its risk difference/relative risk and uncertainty.
- **Why context does not resolve it:** This is the always-register category of a planned primary-outcome analysis not reported. The unadjusted estimate cannot be assumed to be the stratification-only estimate.
- **Supported alternatives / classification ambiguity:** The detailed SAP may have intentionally superseded the extra protocol analysis, or the stratification-only results may exist outside the supplied reports. Neither explanation is an explicit cancellation/report counterpart in the supplied package. This is not a claim that the fully adjusted primary estimate differs from its SAP specification.
- **Display precision:** Not a numeric-copy comparison; the reproduced issue is the adjustment-set requirement and missing matched analysis.
- **Exact human question:** Was the final protocol's stratification-only90d mortality analysis performed, where is it reported, or was its removal from the reporting plan explicitly documented?

### F-XS-002 — Missing hospital-stay censoring time is one hour in the main report and one day in the results supplement

- **Printed values:** Main footnoteb applies to ICU AND acute-hospital duration: patients with missing time to discharge were included as censored **1hour** after randomization. eTable7 lists1653 missingICU durations and1677 missinghospital durations; footnotee specifies **1hour for ICU** and **1day for acute hospital**.
- **Comparison logic / calculation:** Match the same missing acute-hospital-duration observations and survival model. One day is24hours; the main and supplement therefore prescribe different censoring times for those1677 observations. These are explicit analysis-time cutoffs, not imprecise rounded measurements. With exact operational cutoffs the gap is23h.
- **Focus area:** C. **Proposed review domain:** `statistical_results`. **Relationship type:** `S_C`. **All relationships:** `S_C`.
- **Qualifying route:** Route B.
- **Affected result or extracted value:** Acute-hospital-duration Fine–Gray sHR0.98 (0.94,1.02), P.27, and the censoring definition required to reproduce or extract its analysis population and follow-up.
- **Why context does not resolve it:** Neither footnote states that the other is a unit conversion or a different analysis. The hospital-duration endpoint uses calendar days, but that does not make1hour equal1day or identify which cutoff generated the printed sHR.
- **Supported alternatives:** The main may intend1day only for the hospital endpoint, or the supplement may intend1hour for both; the actual analysis code or an explicit clarification would distinguish them. The identical ICU1h descriptions do not settle the hospital definition.
- **Exact human question:** Were missing acute-hospital discharge times censored at1hour or1day after randomization in the reported duration model?

### F-XS-003 — Reported censoring replaces the final SAP's stated imputation rule for missing secondary stay outcomes

Its computational alternatives reduce imputed datasets to5 with1000 bootstrap replications, or give imputedOR and complete-caseRD/OR. The named computational alternatives concern imputation counts/bootstrap or RD/OR presentation, not an exception allowing early censoring of duration outcomes. Full plan mapping contains no explicit missing-duration exception.
- **Focus area:** D. **Proposed review domain:** `protocol_report`. **Relationship type:** `P_R`. **All relationships:** `P_R`.

- **Why context does not resolve it:** The report explains the implemented handling but does not reconcile it with the prospective missing-secondary-outcome rule. The Fine–Gray method's ordinary competing-event censoring does not itself supply the stated synthetic early times for outcomes unavailable due to consent/data loss.
- **Supported alternatives / classification ambiguity:** The SAP's broad MICE paragraph may have been intended only for discrete outcomes, with duration censoring treated as an implicit survival-analysis exception. The supplied final SAP does not state that exception. A later analysis amendment may exist outside the supplied package. ### F-XS-004 — Final protocol prescribes Wilcoxon stay comparisons while final SAP and report use Fine–Gray regression

- **Compared locations and roles:** PLAN [final protocolp70, §4.2.2](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=70) continuing [p71](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=71); PLAN [final SAPp128, §5.3.4](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=128); REPORT [mainp3, Statistical Analysis](../../../jama_martin_2025_oi_250042_1753377747.91025.pdf#page=3), [p8, Table2](../../../jama_martin_2025_oi_250042_1753377747.91025.pdf#page=8). Repeated PLAN locations: [original protocolp30](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=30) and [original SAPp104](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=104).
- **Printed statements:** Final protocol says ICU/acute-hospital duration analyses will use **Wilcoxon rank-sum tests, stratified by survival status**. Final SAP prescribes proportional-hazards regression using **Fine and Gray** for duration among survivors with death as competing risk. The report explicitly uses Fine–Gray and prints sHR1.00 (0.96,1.04), P.97 forICU and0.98 (0.94,1.02), P.27 forhospital. No supplied report prints a Wilcoxon test counterpart.
- **Comparison logic:** Same endpoints and randomized contrast, but a survival-status-stratified rank-sum comparison and adjusted competing-risk regression are distinct methods with distinct inferential quantities. This difference remains between the retained FINAL protocol and FINAL SAP; it is not merely an earlier superseded-protocol version. The final SAP/report agree with each other.
- **Focus area:** D. **Proposed review domain:** `protocol_report`. **Relationship type:** `P_R`. **All relationships:** `P_R, P_P`.
- **Qualifying route:** Route B.
- **Affected result or extracted value:** The prespecified test corresponding to the reportedICU/hospital-duration sHRs and P values, and whether these constitute replacement of the protocol comparisons.
- **Why context does not resolve it:** The package does not explicitly reconcile the retained final-protocol test with the different final-SAP test. The protocol's statement that analyses will be lodged in a SAP supports a possible planned refinement, but does not by itself say the explicitly named rank-sum analysis was replaced or retained in addition.
- **Supported alternatives / classification ambiguity:** The SAP may be intended to supersede the protocol's less detailed analysis text; alternatively rank-sum comparisons may have been performed but omitted. The discrepancy has both PLAN-versus-PLAN and PLAN-versus-REPORT relationships; precedence selectsP_R. No claim is made that Fine–Gray is an unsuitable preferred method.
- **Display precision:** Logical method comparison, no rounding calculation applies.
- **Exact human question:** Was the final protocol's survival-status-stratified Wilcoxon comparison replaced by the Fine–Gray analysis, and where is that replacement or the planned Wilcoxon result documented?
