REPORT exposure key: patient-level median hourly measurement, summarized by group mean(SD) and median(IQR), overall and conditional on supplemental oxygen FiO2>0.21; window from randomization through first10days or earlier refusal, ICU discharge/death. Four duration keys: room air with SpO2>92%; supplemental oxygen with SpO2>92%; target88–92%; below88%. Total supplemental dose is100%-equivalent hours: room air0;1hour FiO2=1 equals2hours FiO2=.605. This implies integrand (FiO2−.21)/.79 over time, although source gives equivalence examples rather than the algebraic formula. Absolute C−U mean differences and percent differences use usual group reference. PaO2 mmHg→kPa multiply.133. Adherence: potential deviation requires FiO2>.21, SpO2>92% and no reduction for ≥3 consecutive hours; site justification and Trial Management Group review; no adherence assessment in usual arm.

REPORT consent key: primary-only alive/dead90days data may remain despite no further consent, while secondary outcomes require linkage/identifiers. Data withdrawn entirely are excluded. This is essential for comparing different outcome denominators. Death-date source precedence: sites after validation, otherwise deaths in either linked source accepted with registration dates paramount. Primary90day all-cause death; ICU/hospital discharge mortality censored90days. ICU death before discharge to level<2 care; hospital follow through acute transfers. One-year binary analysis excludes those not reaching1year before final90day trial end, while eFigure5 time-to-event uses shorter followup censoring. ICU stay sums subsequent ICU admissions within same acute stay, using linear date/time; hospital stay uses calendar dates. Organ-support ordinal score: death≤30days=−1; surviving free days based on respiratory/cardiovascular/renal support each calendar day1–30, days out of ICU free. Older CMP3.1 assumes support at start and maximum of support totals (capped30), not sum.

## Clinical outcomes and methods

Primary endpoint (p124): all-cause death by 90 days after randomization. Interim-only possible substitute is acute-hospital mortality censored at 90 days if death registration linkage has substantial delay. Death-date precedence (p125): sites validate discrepancies and validated site data paramount; if sites cannot validate, death in either linked source counts and death registration determines discrepant dates.

Secondary endpoint list (pp125–126): ICU mortality before discharge to care below Level 2; acute-hospital death includes transfer follow-up until leaving acute hospital; both censored at 90 days. All-cause mortality at 60 days and one year; participants not reaching one-year follow-up before trial closes at final participant's day 90 excluded from one-year analysis. ICU stay is linear time from randomization to discharge/death plus later ICU admissions during same acute-hospital stay. Hospital stay uses calendar days from randomization to discharge/death. Organ-support-free endpoint is ordinal composite: death by day 30 score −1; survivors ranked by calendar days without respiratory, cardiovascular or renal support during days 1–30, randomization day counts as day 1, outside ICU assumed free. For CMP version 3.1 aggregate-only data assume all support at start, take maximum of the three organ-support durations, capped at 30. HRQoL at 90 days EQ-5D-5L collected only among enhanced-subset survivors, target 2500 (15%).

Primary adjusted analysis (pp126–127): site and diagnostic strata plus age, baseline SpO2 and PaO2/FiO2 each restricted cubic spline with 3 knots, COVID-19 indicator, randomization date aggregated in 2-week periods with 7-knot restricted cubic spline. Date×COVID interaction explored. Estimand: all enrolled except complete withdrawals, 90-day mortality variable, adjusted RD summary, treatment-policy strategy for loss to follow-up. Conditional logistic regression with normally distributed random site effects and fixed baseline effects; marginal predictions to whole cohort under all-conservative and all-usual assignments give RD. Percentile 95% CI from 2000 bootstrap samples at 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles. Conditional OR and marginal RR provided additionally.

Confirmatory subgroups (p127): four diagnostic strata (HIE expected greater conservative benefit; sepsis and acute brain injury excluding HIE less benefit; none greater benefit); COVID positive versus not (greater conservative benefit in COVID); ethnicity (possible interaction, direction unclear). Repeat primary model with treatment×subgroup interaction; dummy interactions and joint interaction test if >2 categories; report subgroup ORs, RDs, CIs and interaction P. Exploratory HTE (pp127–128): causal forest in R `grf`, first phase these confirmatory covariates, second phase all baseline/eCRF/CMP covariates prioritized by clinical input.
