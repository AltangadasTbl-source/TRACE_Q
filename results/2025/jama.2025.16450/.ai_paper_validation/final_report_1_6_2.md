## C001 — Moderate-hyperglycemia qualification pathway differs across report and plan descriptions

**Inclusion audit:** CONFIRMED

**Qualifying route:** Route B

**Classification rationale:** Main/results passages report observed trial outcomes; manual/SAP passages prescribe prospective ascertainment. The reported definition differs from PLAN definitions, and the manual narrative differs from its appendix and SAP. P_R takes precedence over P_P. The results supplement corroborates the reported number and supplies no additional conflicting REPORT relationship.

**Classification ambiguity:** The source roles are clear, but no supplied priority rule or amendment establishes whether the shorter appendix/SAP lists abbreviate the full operational narrative. Associated severe and life-threatening branch descriptions are retained as pattern locations, without independently treating those nonkey categories as qualifying outcomes.

**Exact source locations:** [Main PDF p. 1, abstract](../jama_ambalavanan_2025_oi_250072_1761000786.6838.pdf#page=1); [main PDF p. 3, Outcomes](../jama_ambalavanan_2025_oi_250072_1761000786.6838.pdf#page=3); [main PDF p. 6, Safety Outcomes](../jama_ambalavanan_2025_oi_250072_1761000786.6838.pdf#page=6); [results PDF p. 5, eTable 3 hyperglycemia](../joi250072supp4_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf#page=5); [manual PDF p. 39, moderate, severe and life-threatening hyperglycemia narrative](../joi250072supp2_prod_1761000786.6938.pdf#page=39); [manual PDF p. 59, Appendix A hyperglycemia row](../joi250072supp2_prod_1761000786.6938.pdf#page=59); [SAP PDF p. 11, §3.2.4.1](../joi250072supp3_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf#page=11).

**Source evidence:** Moderate glucose is 120–180 mg/dL. The report/manual narrative requires two consecutive determinations at least six hours apart OR more than two determinations within 24 hours without intervening normal values. The manual appendix prints the two-consecutive/separated-determination branch without the additional OR branch; the SAP prints two determinations at least six hours apart without that branch or an explicit consecutive qualifier. **Reported-versus-comparator:** REPORT/main and PLAN/manual narrative contain the additional readings-within-24-hours pathway; PLAN/manual appendix and SAP do not. Associated severe (>180 mg/dL) and life-threatening (>600 mg/dL) narrative descriptions also include the alternative readings branch, while their appendix/SAP descriptions omit it. Severe descriptions additionally refer to insulin initiation, and life-threatening separated readings use four hours. These associated locations do not establish independent numerical consequences.

**Reasoning procedure:** Read the complete moderate criterion lists and compare their logical membership conditions. Tie the comparison to the named quantitative result in the abstract, which meets the workflow's key-result tier even though clinically classified as safety. Separate directly printed differences from possible implementation explanations.

**Calculation:** Three qualifying readings at hours 0, 1 and 2 with no intervening normal value meet the narrative's more-than-two-within-24-hours branch but do not provide two readings at least six hours apart. This is a hypothetical logical counterexample, not an observed participant. Display-precision arithmetic does not apply to the presence of a qualification branch; no discrepancy in the reported RR or CI is asserted.

**Affected result or extracted value:** The event definition associated with 214 versus 156 participants, 66.7% versus 49.8%, and adjusted RR 1.33 (1.17–1.51).

**Why context does not resolve it:** Complete printed criteria remain non-equivalent, and the supplied package does not state which version generated the counts or how the narrative and appendix/SAP lists were reconciled. The difference can alter endpoint membership and therefore the meaning of the named abstract result; its numerical impact is unavailable.

**Alternative source-grounded interpretations:** The narrative may give the full operational definition and the appendix/SAP may summarize it. A source-priority or amendment instruction could also reconcile the lists. Neither interpretation is explicitly established by the supplied package.

**Mechanical evidence recheck:** Every cited location and definition matched its supplied PDF page. The independent rechecker reproduced the logical comparison and confirmed both document roles, P_R/P_P precedence and protocol_report classification. Participant-level glucose readings, measurement times and event adjudication records are not supplied; no observed reclassification or effect-size change is inferred.

**Potential downstream evidence impact:** If confirmed, an extractor could associate the reported hyperglycemia RR with different ascertainment criteria depending on which supplied definition is copied. The package does not establish that any downstream synthesis or clinical conclusion changed.
