## Tooling and preprocessing

Direct pdfinfo, pdftotext, pdftoppm/pdftocairo, tesseract and libreoffice were unavailable. PDF information, native/layout text and rendering used the permitted package-local helper with pinned PyMuPDF 1.26.4 and pypdf 5.9.0; rendering ran on CPU. User-approved RapidOCR CUDA used the existing stt environment, rapidocr-onnxruntime 1.4.4 and onnxruntime-gpu 1.28.0 on an RTX 5070 Laptop GPU. The temporary fallback environment is removed by the coordinator during finalization; the user environment is retained.

The initial CUDA-12-path test produced CPU-fallback warnings, preserved in its log. Corrected CUDA-13 production logs show no fallback, and PowerShell GPU observation recorded utilization. Three unusable reusable renders—protocol pages 23 and 27 and manual page 119—were preserved, freshly rendered from source and successfully reprocessed. All affected units were mapped. Manual PDF page 153 is blank apart from its folio; derivative provenance headers are not source content. There are 245 page-scoped PLAN OCR output records, covering 241 fresh-required pages plus four overlaps, not 245 nonblank extracted pages.

OCR and derivative text can contain recognition or font-encoding limitations; they were locators and transcription aids. Source visuals and complete source-linked maps resolved relevant reading gaps. No candidate relies on an unconfirmed OCR transcription.
