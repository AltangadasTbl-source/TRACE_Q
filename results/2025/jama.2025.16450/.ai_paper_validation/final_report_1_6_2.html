C001 — Moderate-hyperglycemia qualification pathway differs across report and plan descriptions

Status: Pending Human Adjudication

Relationship type: P_R

The reported definition differs from PLAN definitions, and the manual narrative differs from its appendix and SAP. P_R takes precedence over P_P. Exact source locations: Main PDF p. 1, abstract; main PDF p. 3, Outcomes; main PDF p. 6, Safety Outcomes; results PDF p. 5, eTable 3 hyperglycemia; manual PDF p. 39, moderate, severe and life-threatening hyperglycemia narrative; manual PDF p. 59, Appendix A hyperglycemia row; SAP PDF p. 11, §3.2.4.1.

Source evidence: Moderate glucose is 120–180 mg/dL. The report/manual narrative requires two consecutive determinations at least six hours apart OR more than two determinations within 24 hours without intervening normal values. The manual appendix prints the two-consecutive/separated-determination branch without the additional OR branch; the SAP prints two determinations at least six hours apart without that branch or an explicit consecutive qualifier. Reasoning procedure: Read the complete moderate criterion lists and compare their logical membership conditions. Tie the comparison to the named quantitative result in the abstract, which meets the workflow's key-result tier even though clinically classified as safety. Separate directly printed differences from possible implementation explanations.

Calculation: Three qualifying readings at hours 0, 1 and 2 with no intervening normal value meet the narrative's more-than-two-within-24-hours branch but do not provide two readings at least six hours apart. This is a hypothetical logical counterexample, not an observed participant. Display-precision arithmetic does not apply to the presence of a qualification branch; no discrepancy in the reported RR or CI is asserted.

Affected result or extracted value: The event definition associated with 214 versus 156 participants, 66.7% versus 49.8%, and adjusted RR 1.33 (1.17–1.51).

Why context does not resolve it: Complete printed criteria remain non-equivalent, and the supplied package does not state which version generated the counts or how the narrative and appendix/SAP lists were reconciled. Alternative source-grounded interpretations: The narrative may give the full operational definition and the appendix/SAP may summarize it. A source-priority or amendment instruction could also reconcile the lists. Neither interpretation is explicitly established by the supplied package.
