The hash list is intentionally broader than usable scientific transcription: partial derivatives and document metadata are retained for identity and gap tracing.

Fitness meanings: USABLE supports its stated locations; PARTIAL supports only part of that scope or is low-resolution; STALE refers to superseded coverage descriptions; DUPLICATE is a lower-priority parallel representation; UNREADABLE supplies no readable evidence. Native and OCR text are aids; exact direct-source pages remain authority. An absent file is not inferred from former review scope.

No new OCR, render, or text derivative was generated by this curator. Direct PDF information was obtained with the supplied fallback helper and pinned PyMuPDF1.26.4. Hashes use direct sha256sum.
