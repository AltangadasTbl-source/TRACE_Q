- **Printed statements:** SAP “midline shift > 5mm”; main “5 mm or greater”; results and protocol ≥5 mm. eTable 2 prints shift-component cases 11 versus 17, total 28.
- **Comparison logic:** In otherwise matched six-month homolateral primary recurrence definitions, an exactly 5-mm asymptomatic recurrence qualifies by the inclusive rule and fails the strict rule unless another component applies. The symbol is visually confirmed in the SAP PDF, not inferred from OCR. Display precision does not apply to a categorical inclusion operator.
- **Focus area:** D. **Proposed review domain:** protocol_report. **Primary relationship:** P_R. **All relationships:** P_R, P_P. The original protocol also independently conflicts with the SAP endpoint definition.
- **Qualifying route:** Route A, category 5.

- **Why context does not resolve it:** Category 5 applies to a change in a composite definition. No supplied amendment specifies which boundary should govern adjudication; matching the other three components does not determine the boundary.
- **Supported alternatives:** The SAP strict operator could represent a textual simplification while the inclusive protocol/report rule governed adjudication, or the strict SAP rule could have governed analysis. No number of exactly 5-mm participants is supplied; no changed count is asserted.
- **Human question:** Which midline-shift boundary governed adjudication and final primary analysis, and did any exactly 5-mm case qualify solely under this component?

Original protocol specifies Mantel–Haenszel adjusted for center upon model nonconvergence. Report says center was planned but not introduced because estimated random-effect variance was zero; displayed primary model adjusts medication and laterality.
- **Comparison logic:** The covariates and ITT/imputation specification match, but the reported final model replaces the planned mixed-model center component with no center component. The zero-variance explanation is explicit and is retained. It is not the original protocol's named nonconvergence fallback. No numerical difference in OR or variance is inferred; display precision is inapplicable to the analysis specification.
- **Focus area:** D. **Proposed review domain:** protocol_report. **Primary relationship / All relationships:** P_R.
- **Qualifying route:** Route A, category 5.
- **Affected result or extracted value:** Primary adjusted imputed OR .64 (.36–1.14), P=.13 and bootstrap risk difference −.06 (−.14 to .02), as produced by the final model.
- **Why context does not resolve it:** Category 5 registers a planned primary analysis replaced by a reported specification; the disclosed zero-variance rationale is an alternative explanation rather than evidence that the supplied plans contain this replacement rule.
- **Supported alternatives:** A fitted mixed model with estimated random-effect variance exactly zero may yield the same estimates as the reported fixed-effects model; the omission may be an implementation representation of that boundary fit. The supplied results do not establish different estimates or a failure to fit the planned model.
- **Human question:** Was the reported primary inference obtained directly from a fitted zero-variance mixed model or from a refitted model without center, and what prespecified or amended rule governed that implementation?
