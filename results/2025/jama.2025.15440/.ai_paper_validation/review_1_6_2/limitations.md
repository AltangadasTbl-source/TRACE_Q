# Limitations and missing definitions — Workflow 1.6.2

All 70 supplied physical PDF pages were mapped and reviewed. There are no scientific source-unit coverage gaps. Thirty-four pages had reusable coverage; all 36 fresh-required pages were mapped directly. The following limitations concern absent underlying evidence or definitions, rather than omitted supplied units.

- Protocol Table 4 does not identify the exact power software, version, function, correction convention or underlying unrounded rate/subgroup inputs. The diagnostic method for C001 is supported by all 23 other power cells, source-grounded equal allocation and continuity-correction context. Its full conservative input enclosure remains separated from printed 34%; no replacement power or preferred analysis is assigned.
- Individual and imputed AF-days and oral-anticoagulation-month outcomes, permutation distributions/replication details and exact mean/difference confidence-interval construction are not supplied. Available point containment, interval ordering, matching definitions and interval/test implications were checked without assuming an unreported implementation.
- Log-rank observed-minus-expected quantities and variances, event/censoring histories and exact quantile confidence-interval procedures are absent. Consequently, exact time-to-event estimates cannot be independently recreated from aggregate event counts alone; crude proportions remain distinct from censored estimates.
- Exact ordinal scores, continuous-test implementation and individual ranks are not supplied for every supplementary comparison. Reproducing categorical tests and bounded continuous/trend diagnostics were distinguished from exact replication.
- Histogram bars do not print individual bin counts. All panels, axes, populations and printed grouped percentages were mapped visually; exact unlabeled counts were not inferred from pixel heights.
- Separately referenced outcome-derivation and health-economic documents, and a later detailed clinical-analysis specification, are not supplied. They were not reconstructed or sought externally. Their absence alone is not a candidate.
- Some retained native/OCR/rendered derivatives lack original engine versions and retain partial or unreadable text. Their fitness and hashes are recorded in evidence_asset_inventory.md. Fresh extraction and source-page inspection closed every affected coverage gap. Approved RapidOCR CUDA text was a locator; source visuals were authoritative for candidate transcription.

The review is quantitative reporting quality control using supplied package evidence. It does not independently establish raw-data accuracy, assess general methodological or clinical merit, or determine whether any observed treatment conclusion changed. C001 remains Pending Human Adjudication; its downstream relevance is limited to prospective subgroup-power extraction. Final environment cleanup, integrity verification and automated validation are documented separately by the coordinator in tooling_record.md and review_validation.json.
