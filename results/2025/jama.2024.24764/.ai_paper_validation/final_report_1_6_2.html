Reasoning procedure: Make the literal fixed-indicator interpretation explicit, test its column relationships, then separate dependence of individual coefficients from estimability of contrasts of fitted means. Preserve alternate subject-identifier or constrained-parameter meanings rather than treating omitted syntax alone as a candidate.

Calculation: For participant indicator columns Iᵢ, time-invariant treatment Tᵢ and baseline score Bᵢ give T=ΣᵢTᵢIᵢ and B=ΣᵢBᵢIᵢ. Thus treatment and baseline lie in the participant-column span. Separate unrestricted coefficients are not identified under this literal design. Some group-average contrasts of fitted responses can remain estimable and invariant to this redistribution, and a time×treatment contrast can remain estimable. This exact conditional identity is not a printed numeric recomputation; rounding/display-precision intervals are inapplicable.

Why context does not resolve it: B1: positive prospective parameter-role/method logic falls within focus C/D. B2: the all-fixed parenthesis does not distinguish a participant indicator from a repeated-subject identifier or constrained parameter, a distinction material to separate baseline/treatment adjustment. B3: the intended adjusted contrast cannot be identified from the printed specification alone. The candidate is bounded to this ambiguity.

Alternative source-grounded interpretations: Participant may identify the repeated subject rather than a fitted fixed effect. A constrained/reparameterized model or an estimable marginal contrast combining participant and treatment terms may have been intended. The prospective repeated-model language permits these possibilities, while the all-fixed declaration does not select one. No universal contrast-inestimability claim or preferred alternative analysis is made.
