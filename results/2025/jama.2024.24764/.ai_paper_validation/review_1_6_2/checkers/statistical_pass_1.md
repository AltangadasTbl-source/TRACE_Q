### F-ST1-003 — Literal fixed participant parameters do not identify the stated absolute group contrasts

**Focus area:** C and D.  
**Proposed review domain:** plan_material.  
**Proposed relationship type / all relationships:** P_P / P_P.  
**Qualifying route:** Route B.

**Printed statements:** The paragraph includes participant, treatment group, baseline score, time and minimisation parameters, “all as fixed effects except centre which will be included as a random effect.” All baseline/posttreatment times are included; a time×treatment term permits different treatment effects over time; the stated target is group mean differences at relevant times.

**Rule and calculation:** If participant means a fixed indicator for each participant, treatment allocation is time-invariant within participant. Thus treatment column T equals the sum of fixed participant indicators for participants allocated iNPWT. Baseline score B equals Σ B_i times the corresponding participant indicator. Both columns are in the span of the participant columns. The unconstrained treatment main effect and baseline-score coefficient are not jointly identified with all participant fixed effects. Time×treatment differences can still represent changes relative to a time reference, but those alone do not supply the absolute time-specific treatment mean differences specified in the paragraph. This is a design-matrix identity, not a numerical approximation or critique of a coherent stated modeling choice.

**Affected result or extracted value:** The planned time-specific treatment mean differences for PCS/MCS and EQ-5D index/health, including the baseline-adjusted repeated-model estimand.

**Why context does not resolve it:** B1: the specified repeated-model parameter roles create a statistical-method identification gap. B2: the text explicitly calls participant fixed while stating treatment and baseline fixed too; it gives no identifiability constraint or different meaning of participant. B3: this prevents determining which stated model is intended to yield the reported absolute contrasts. No fitted-data claim is made.

**Alternatives and missing definition:** “Participant” may instead denote the subject identifier for repeated covariance, a random participant intercept, or a constrained design yielding specified contrasts. Those are possible interpretations; the supplied paragraph does not distinguish them. The report does not explicitly restate fixed participant effects, so it is not added as a conflicting REPORT party. No replacement method is proposed.

**Exact human question:** Does “participant” denote fixed participant indicators, a repeated-subject identifier, or another parameterization, and what specification identifies the stated absolute treatment mean differences?
