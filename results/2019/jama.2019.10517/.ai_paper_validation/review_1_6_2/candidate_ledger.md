## C001 — Primary relapse score component differs from the plan

**Qualifying route:** Route A, category 5.

**Candidate statement:** The report lists HDRS ≥18 as a sufficient primary-relapse score component, whereas the plan also requires a 5-point increase relative to RCT entry.

**Exact source locations:** PLAN [protocol p.12, section 3.9.8, lines 553-568](../../joi190079supp1_prod.pdf#page=12); REPORT [main p.4, Primary Outcome: Relapse](../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=4). Affected primary results: [abstract p.1](../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=1), [primary results p.6](../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=6), [Table 3 p.7](../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=7).

**Source evidence and reasoning:** Plan HAM-D ≥18 AND a mean absolute increase of 5 points on at least one visit relative to RCT entry; report HDRS ≥18 without the conjunction. Both refer to the same randomized-phase 17-item depression score. Diagnostic example only: entry 15 to 18 is a 3-point rise and satisfies only the printed report component; no observed event is reassigned. This is a definition comparison, so numeric display precision does not apply. The SADS ≥3 confirmation remains operational source context, and psychiatric-hospitalization reasons reconcile through the Table 3 footnote; neither is a separate issue.

**Affected result or extracted value:** Operational primary relapse composite underlying 13/64 versus 34/62 and adjusted HR 0.25 (95% CI 0.13-0.48).

**Why context does not resolve it:** Route A category 5 covers a changed printed primary composite even if an unchanged operational rule was abbreviated.

**Alternative source-grounded interpretations:** Report abbreviation of an unchanged rule, or an amended score component; supplied evidence does not establish which.

**Classification rationale:** Prospective primary definition conflicts with the reported sufficient-condition list; P_R, protocol_report. **Classification ambiguity:** None as to document roles; operational abbreviation/amendment remains the human question.

**Exact remaining human question:** Was the additional 5-point HDRS increase from RCT entry required to classify the primary relapses that generated the printed counts and HR?

**Provenance:** F-XS-001 cross_source_consistency_reviewer and F-ST1-001 statistics_pass_1; independent screen INCLUDE for both.

## C003 — Adverse-event baseline differs between plan and report

**Qualifying route:** Route B.

**Candidate statement:** The plan restricts phase-specific adverse-event incidence to participants not meeting the specified criteria at acute-phase baseline, while the report defines UKU increases and incident akathisia using randomized-phase baseline conditions.

**Exact source locations:** PLAN [p.15, Non-Metabolic Safety Data, lines 705-717](../../joi190079supp1_prod.pdf#page=15); REPORT [main p.4, Other Measures of Tolerability](../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=4), [p.7, adverse-effect/akathisia results](../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=7).

**Source evidence and reasoning:** Acute baseline precedes up to 12 weeks acute treatment and 8 weeks stabilization; RCT baseline follows these phases. The PLAN explicitly requires absence of its adverse-event criteria at acute-phase baseline for incidence eligibility. Its generic UKU phrase “from baseline” alone does not specify an epoch and is not the comparison anchor. The REPORT explicitly uses RCT-baseline conditions for UKU increase and incident akathisia; score-4 wording is not an independent issue. No raw participant trajectories establish changed event counts; display precision does not apply to epoch definitions.

**Affected result or extracted value:** Reported UKU frequencies, including sedation 7.8%/3.2%, dizziness 7.8%/3.2%, nausea/vomiting 3.1%/8.1%, and incident akathisia 4.7%/4.8%.

**Why context does not resolve it:** Different definite baseline rules alter the meaning of the extracted incident-event percentage; no supplied amendment identifies which prospective rule governed it.

**Alternative source-grounded interpretations:** Intentional randomized-phase incidence definition or changed prospective rule. No preferred reference epoch is proposed.

**Classification rationale:** PLAN versus REPORT safety endpoint definition; P_R, protocol_report. **Classification ambiguity:** None as to roles; effect on individual counts unavailable.

**Exact remaining human question:** Were these incidences defined relative to acute or RCT baseline, and how is the reported baseline reconciled with the supplied plan?

**Provenance:** F-XS-005 cross_source_consistency_reviewer; independently INCLUDE.

## C005 — Planned metabolic observation exclusion is reported as sensitivity analysis

**Qualifying route:** Route B.

**Candidate statement:** The plan places exclusion of post-hyperlipidemia-treatment observations in the H2 mixed model, while the report describes that operation within a post hoc sensitivity analysis compared with all-participant analyses.

**Exact source locations:** PLAN [p.14, H2, lines 673-679](../../joi190079supp1_prod.pdf#page=14); REPORT [main abstract p.1](../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=1), [statistical sensitivity methods p.5](../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=5), [secondary/drug-change results p.7](../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=7).

**Source evidence and reasoning:** Plan says pertinent later metabolic values will be excluded from the mixed model after hyperlipidemia treatment starts/changes. Report calls exclusion after statin/hypoglycemic starts/changes post hoc sensitivity; 12 participants had statin changes, 6 per arm, and sensitivity results were qualitatively similar to all-participant analyses. “All participants” does not establish that all observations were included; the printed principal-versus-sensitivity placement is the comparison anchor. The additional hypoglycemic extension is not asserted to have been planned. The counterpart is described, so the issue is principal-versus-sensitivity specification rather than absent-analysis pattern C002. No numerical difference is imputed and display precision is not applicable.

**Affected result or extracted value:** Observation rule and prespecification role behind the headline cholesterol/triglyceride treatment-by-time estimates.

**Why context does not resolve it:** Qualitative similarity does not identify which observation rule generated the principal reported coefficients or reconcile the planned exclusion's analysis role.

**Alternative source-grounded interpretations:** Unprovided amendment; or post hoc refers only to extending a retained planned statin rule to hypoglycemic drugs.

**Classification rationale:** PLAN main-model rule compared with REPORT sensitivity methods/results; P_R, protocol_report. **Classification ambiguity:** Whether the wording applies to the entire operation or only its extension remains unspecified.

**Exact remaining human question:** Did the principal metabolic estimates exclude post-hyperlipidemia-treatment observations as planned, or was this operation moved to sensitivity analysis?

**Provenance:** F-XS-003 cross_source_consistency_reviewer and F-ST1-004 statistics_pass_1; both independently INCLUDE.
