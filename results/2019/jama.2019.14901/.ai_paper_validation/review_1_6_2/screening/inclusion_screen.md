PLAN [protocol p. 33, section 6.2](../../../joi190108supp1_prod.pdf#page=33) explicitly proceeds from univariate predictors of reintubation to multivariate logistic regression, a maximal model with dependent-variable association P<.20 and relevant forced variables, then descending elimination retaining dependent-variable P<.05 and forced variables. REPORT [main p. 4, Statistical Analysis](../../../jama_thille_2019_oi_190108.pdf#page=4) explicitly says all analyses used a predefined statistical analysis plan, but then specifies adjustment for PaCO2 and baseline imbalance defined as P<.05. REPORT [main p. 7](../../../jama_thille_2019_oi_190108.pdf#page=7) identifies the chronic-lung-disease adjustment and OR 0.60 (95% CI 0.38–0.93), P=.02. These are differing covariate-selection predicates, rather than rounded copies or different names for the same population. Classification is P_R / protocol_report.

A separate prognostic modeling exercise or an unsupplied SAP/amendment could explain the relationship; the report does not identify that mapping. Category 5 is supported by the explicit predefined-analysis claim and absent report-specific rule in the supplied plan; Route B independently applies because the primary adjusted OR's governing planned covariate-selection specification is unclear. This issue does not compare an adjusted OR with a crude ratio.

Classification is P_R / protocol_report. Primary reintubation and ICU mortality are the qualifying anchors; every mortality-plan location is retained in this one repeated missing-Cox-output pattern. The issue establishes absence from the supplied report package, not that an analysis was never performed.

### Ordinary secondary support-free days: F-ST1-004

PLAN [protocol p. 25, sections 3.3 and 3.5](../../../joi190108supp1_prod.pdf#page=25) explicitly prespecifies ventilatory-support-free days including intubation/NIV/HFNC during 14 days; PLAN [p. 33](../../../joi190108supp1_prod.pdf#page=33) specifies its t/Mann–Whitney comparison. REPORT [main p. 1](../../../jama_thille_2019_oi_190108.pdf#page=1) says 11 prespecified secondary outcomes; the REPORT [main p. 4 list](../../../jama_thille_2019_oi_190108.pdf#page=4), [Table 2 p. 8](../../../jama_thille_2019_oi_190108.pdf#page=8), and REPORT [supplement eTables 3–4 pp. 6–7](../../../joi190108supp2_prod.pdf#page=6) omit support-free days. Whole-source searches find no reported support-free-day result. Thus the omission itself is reproduced.

Neither the plan nor abstract identifies this endpoint as key secondary; the report supplies no competing primary/secondary/tertiary/exploratory/safety assignment or composite change for it. Category 5 distinguishes a prespecified outcome-status change from nonreporting of a planned primary or key-secondary analysis: ordinary-secondary absence alone is not either category. Calling the remaining 11 outcomes prespecified does not state that support-free days received a different hierarchy status. The supplied package also establishes no resulting change in a primary/key outcome, analysis-population size or decision rule. Consequently the comparison-tier gate screens this finding out before Route B; no number-of-outcomes arithmetic is the basis of this decision.

### Key-secondary tachypnea duration: F-ST1-006

PLAN [protocol p. 30, explicit ARF definition](../../../joi190108supp1_prod.pdf#page=30) requires at least two criteria including respiratory rate >25/min during at least two hours. REPORT [main p. 4, explicit postextubation-respiratory-failure definition](../../../jama_thille_2019_oi_190108.pdf#page=4) also requires at least two criteria but prints respiratory rate >25/min without that duration. The result is key secondary because REPORT [abstract p. 1](../../../jama_thille_2019_oi_190108.pdf#page=1) names and reports it; REPORT [Table 2 p. 8](../../../jama_thille_2019_oi_190108.pdf#page=8) prints 88/302 versus 70/339, difference −8.5 (95% CI −15.2 to −1.8), P=.01. The comparator is an explicit planned endpoint window, not a hypothesized unreported model detail. Classification is P_R / protocol_report.

Route B1 holds for a planned/report endpoint definition affecting a named key secondary result. B2 holds because the explicit report definition does not say that its tachypnea criterion incorporates the protocol duration or direct readers to that condition; a condensation versus a changed definition remains unresolved. B3 holds because the duration is part of the classifier an extractor would attach to the reported event counts. No participant-level reclassification is asserted, and no numerical estimate is recomputed; this does not prevent screening a positively stated planned threshold/window against the reported explicit definition. Its decision differs from ordinary-secondary nonreporting because it compares an actually reported key outcome's definition rather than an absent non-key result.
