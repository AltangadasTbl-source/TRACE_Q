## Planned quantitative/statistical relationships

Each S relationship is a mapping unit, not a finding. Matching main-paper keys identify comparisons to be reconstructed by the main mapper/checkers without reading main pages outside this agent's assignment.

### S201 — Primary endpoint and contrast

[Protocol p25](../../../joi190108supp1_prod.pdf#page=25), also p9/p17: superior-effect hypothesis HFNC plus NIV reduces reintubation relative to HFNC alone; primary outcome reintubation within7 days after planned extubation in high-risk ICU patients. Rationale p17 chooses7 rather than48/72 hours to allow for NIV-related delay. Main key: primary endpoint, Table2 primary row, abstract day7 contrast.

### S202 — Every planned secondary outcome/status

[Protocol p25](../../../joi190108supp1_prod.pdf#page=25), French synopsis pp9–10: (1) reintubation48h,72h,up to ICU discharge; (2) ARF criteria within7 days; (3) number continuing allocated HFNC/HFNC+NIV beyond48h; (4) ventilatory-support-free days within14 days, including intubation,NIV,HFNC; (5) ICU and hospital length of stay; (6) mortality ICU,hospital,day28,day90. All are explicitly secondary, with no separately designated key, tertiary, exploratory, or safety outcome hierarchy here. Main keys: outcomes paragraph/Table2; outcome status of continued treatment, support-free days, day28 mortality, composite mortality-or-reintubation, meeting-reintubation-criteria, reintubated-patient mortality.

### S203 — Planned population

[Protocol p26](../../../joi190108supp1_prod.pdf#page=26): mechanical ventilation >24h; successful readiness test/planned extubation; age>65 or underlying chronic cardiac/lung disease. French synopsis p10 says at least24h; screening p27 says at least24h and excludes less than24h. Cardiac: LVEF≤45%, documented ischemic heart disease, chronic atrial fibrillation, prior cardiogenic pulmonary edema. Lung: COPD, obesity-hypoventilation, restrictive disease; documented or suspected with hypercapnic intubation PaCO2>45 plus smoking/intrinsic PEEP/emphysema, BMI>30 with PaCO2>45, or chest-wall deformation. Exclusions: traumatic brain injury, peripheral neuromuscular cause, long-term NIV, long-term CPAP, NIV contraindication, unplanned extubation (later planned eligible), do-not-reintubate, lack insurance, protected populations, opposition. Main key: Eligibility/population/flow exclusion definitions.

### S204 — Randomization and baseline stratification

[Protocol pp28–29](../../../joi190108supp1_prod.pdf#page=29): open label, multicenter, centrally organized1:1 allocation stratified by center and PaCO2. Hypercapnia PaCO2>45 mmHg measured at end of spontaneous breathing trial; if unavailable last blood gas on mechanical ventilation. T-piece or PS7 cmH2O with no PEEP; test30min–2h. Readiness p27: resolving cause, minimal/no vasopressor, awake RASS+1 to−2, no continuous sedation, RR≤35, SpO2>90% on FiO2≤40% or PaO2/FiO2≥150 with PEEP≤8. Main key: stratification, trial modality, hypercapnic subgroup.

### S205 — Intervention dose/duration and extension

[Protocol pp29–30](../../../joi190108supp1_prod.pdf#page=30): both arms≥48h. HFNC continuously50 L/min, FiO2 for SpO2≥92%. NIV immediately, first session≥4h then sessions≥2h, overnight22:00–06:00 if possible, total≥12h/day. PS≥5 targeting tidal volume6–8 mL/kg; PEEP5–10 cmH2O, FiO2 for SpO2≥92%, pressure ramp0.1–0.2s, cycling25–30% peak flow. Between sessions HFNC50 L/min. Recovery at48h permits switch after HFNC trial30 L/min/FiO2 30%; persistent/new ARF leads continuation/reinitiation in24h periods until recovery in allocated arm. Therapeutic NIV discouraged. Main key: interventions and beyond48h outcome.

### S206 — Planned acute respiratory failure

[Protocol p30](../../../joi190108supp1_prod.pdf#page=30): at least2 criteria: RR>25/min for≥2h; distress/increased work/fatigue/accessory muscle use; pH<7.35 AND PaCO2>45; FiO2≥50% to maintain SpO2≥92% OR PaO2/FiO2≤150. Main keys: secondary respiratory-failure definition, eTable1 components.

### S207 — Planned reintubation decision rule

[Protocol pp30–31](../../../joi190108supp1_prod.pdf#page=31): any of respiratory failure with≥2 of RR>35, distress, pH<7.35 plus PaCO2>45, FiO2≥80% to maintain SpO2≥92% or PaO2/FiO2≤100; hemodynamic failure SAP<90 or MAP<65 with vasopressor need; neurological failure Glasgow<12 or agitation; cardiac/respiratory arrest. Immediate intubation once criteria met, although therapeutic NIV according to center practice is allowed; persistent ARF2h after NIV requires immediate intubation and NIV cannot continue beyond2h. Main key: severe respiratory failure/reintubation criteria, eTable1.
