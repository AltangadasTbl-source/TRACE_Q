# Numeric consistency review

## Scope and rule

The complete assigned scope is 30 numeric relationships N001–N013 and N501–N517, together with the numeric fields of all 163 statistical relationships S001–S092 and S501–S571. The source-linked canonical inventories and all three new evidence maps were read. QUALITY_CONTROL_SCOPE.md and workflow_1_6_2/review_contract.md were read completely. Existing review conclusions and external sources were not used. This checker owns only this artifact; the coordinator's stable relationship inventory remains unchanged.

Sources are the main article (REPORT), protocol and SAP (PLAN), and Supplement 3, whose operational toolkit is PLAN and whose trial eTables/eFigures are REPORT. Direct Poppler text and rendered source pages supplied the numeric authority. In particular, main article Tables 1–3 and Figure 3, and Supplement 3 eTables 1, 3, and 4 were visually inspected against their mapped values. Direct source text was used for flow labels and all other printed quantities. No OCR or rendering was required beyond existing CPU derivatives.

For every displayed count/percentage pair, the count n and denominator d were allowed half-unit intervals, and the percentage p its own half-unit interval: recomputed percentage interval = [100(n−0.5)/(d+0.5), 100(n+0.5)/(d−0.5)]. Interval gap is zero on overlap; otherwise it is the distance between nearest limits. Relative discrepancy is gap/absolute printed percentage. A gap above 5% is required by the display-precision rule; interval-versus-test checks are assigned independently to the statistical specialists. Counts also received exact integer sum checks. Apparent effect-measure conflicts were distinguished from comparisons of adjusted estimates with crude counts.

## Limitations and counts

Thirty N relationships and numeric fields of all 163 S relationships were reviewed; 228 explicit count/percentage pairs and all 20 populated flow blocks were checked. Findings: 0. Excluded observations: 12 consolidated rows. All assigned relationship groups received a durable check record above. There is no uncovered assigned numeric unit.

Restricted Table 3 denominator integers and exact sample-size cluster-period assumptions are not supplied; these limitations prevent their precise standalone reconstruction without creating a printed contradiction. Detailed interval/P compatibility, model reproduction and planned-versus-reported design are checked by the independently assigned statistical and cross-source specialists. Source and reusable assets were preserved.
