## Findings

### F-XS-001 — Planned statin component is absent from the reported optimal in-hospital medication composite

- **Exact locations and roles:** PLAN [SAP p.5, section 6.1.2, lines 1149–1151](../../../joi170166supp2_prod.pdf#page=5); REPORT [main p.3, Outcomes](../../../jama_huffman_2018_oi_170166.pdf#page=3), [main p.7, Table 3 optimal in-hospital medication row and footnote c](../../../jama_huffman_2018_oi_170166.pdf#page=7), [supplement p.21, eTable 5 optimal in-hospital medication row and footnote c](../../../joi170166supp3_prod.pdf#page=21), and [supplement p.22, eTable 6 corresponding row and footnote b](../../../joi170166supp3_prod.pdf#page=22).
- **Printed statements and values:** The SAP defines the composite using aspirin, an ADP receptor antagonist, heparin, statin and beta blocker. Main Outcomes instead lists aspirin, an ADP receptor antagonist, anticoagulant and beta blocker, expressly stating that in-hospital statin was predefined but data were not collected. Table 3 reports control 3122 (31.7%) and intervention 3878 (35.8%), primary adjusted difference 6.00 percentage points (95% CI, 3.90–8.11), and OR 1.45 (1.28–1.64). Table 3 and both supplementary sensitivity-table definitions omit statin.
- **Comparison logic:** Item-by-item comparison retains aspirin, ADP antagonist and beta blocker, treats anticoagulant/heparin wording separately, and identifies statin as the absent fifth component. This changes the conjunction defining successful optimal medication use and potentially the patients eligible for every component. It is a categorical composite-definition comparison, so no numerical rounding interval or relative discrepancy applies. The printed result values are associated with the four-component reported definition; no five-component result can be recovered from supplied aggregates.
- **Focus area:** D. **Proposed review domain:** `protocol_report`. **Relationship type:** `P_R`. **All relationships:** `P_R`.
- **Qualifying route:** Route A, category 5.
- **Affected result or extracted value:** Optimal in-hospital medication use and its reported 6.00 percentage-point adjusted difference and 1.45 OR; sensitivity ORs 2.19 (1.83–2.62) with GRACE covariates, 1.47 (1.29–1.66) with transfer status, 1.45 (1.28–1.64) with insurance status, 1.47 (1.30–1.66) with prehospital aspirin, and 0.87 (0.70–1.07) with exposure-time interaction are attached to the reported composite.
- **Why context does not resolve it:** Route A category 5 applies to a changed prespecified composite even when the report explicitly discloses why one component was omitted. The disclosure establishes the changed definition; it does not supply a result for the planned composite.
- **Supported alternatives:** The four-component definition may be an intentional, disclosed adaptation because statin data were unavailable. An additional approved endpoint amendment may exist outside the supplied package. Neither alternative supplies the planned five-component result here.
- **Exact human question:** Should the reported optimal in-hospital medication result be recorded as an amended four-component secondary composite, and was an amendment approved for removing the prespecified statin component and any corresponding eligibility criterion?

### F-XS-002 — The reported prespecified subgroup analyses differ from the supplied a priori list

- **Exact locations and roles:** PLAN [SAP p.7, section 7.5.2, lines 1243–1254](../../../joi170166supp2_prod.pdf#page=7), supported by [SAP p.5, section 6.1.3, lines 1156–1165](../../../joi170166supp2_prod.pdf#page=5); REPORT [main p.3, Statistical Analysis subgroup sentence](../../../jama_huffman_2018_oi_170166.pdf#page=3), [main p.9, Figure 3 title, age and hospital-type rows, and accompanying subgroup paragraph](../../../jama_huffman_2018_oi_170166.pdf#page=9), and [main p.10, opening Discussion paragraph](../../../jama_huffman_2018_oi_170166.pdf#page=10).
- **Printed statements and values:** The SAP a priori list contains hospital size, use of quality-improvement toolkit components, age less than 65 years and greater than 65 years, sex, and STEMI versus NSTEMI. The report calls age, sex, STEMI/NSTEMI, hospital size and hospital type prespecified. Figure 3 uses age less than 50, 50–69 and at least 70 years, with adjusted ORs 1.53 (0.83–2.79), 0.89 (0.67–1.19) and 0.98 (0.71–1.35); it reports government, nonprofit and private hospital-type ORs 0.90 (0.61–1.33), 0.91 (0.59–1.40) and 1.16 (0.82–1.64). No toolkit-use subgroup result is present in the article or results supplement.
- **Comparison logic:** The list was matched item by item. Sex, STEMI/NSTEMI and hospital size match. Age cutpoints do not match. Hospital type is absent from the complete supplied prospective subgroup list despite the repeated prespecified label. Toolkit-component use is a planned subgroup of the primary MACE analysis, but no corresponding result appears in any REPORT content. The main p.3 statement that the mixed-methods process evaluation will be reported separately is retained as an alternative rather than assumed to contain this primary-outcome subgroup result. This is a planned-analysis specification and status comparison; rounding intervals and relative numerical discrepancies do not apply to the categorical subgroup definitions.
- **Focus area:** D. **Proposed review domain:** `protocol_report`. **Relationship type:** `P_R`. **All relationships:** `P_R`.
- **Qualifying route:** Route A, category 5.
- **Affected result or extracted value:** Primary 30-day MACE heterogeneity analyses, the six age/hospital-type ORs above, and the characterization of Figure 3 and the narrative as prespecified; the planned toolkit-use subgroup analysis has no extracted result in this package.
- **Why context does not resolve it:** Route A category 5 applies to reported analyses described as prespecified but absent in the supplied plan and to replaced or absent planned primary-outcome analyses. The reported cutpoints define different patient groups; hospital type and hospital size are separate variables in Figure 3, so one cannot substitute for the other.
- **Supported alternatives:** A later prespecified amendment could have revised the age cutpoints and added hospital type. The age and hospital-type analyses could instead be additional analyses with the prespecified label applying too broadly. Toolkit-use analyses could be deferred to the separately described process evaluation. The supplied package does not document any such amendment or primary-MACE toolkit-use result.
- **Exact human question:** Which dated plan or amendment prespecified the reported age cutpoints and hospital-type analysis, and where are the SAP-specified age-at-65 and toolkit-component-use analyses of primary 30-day MACE reported or explained as replaced or deferred?

### F-XS-003 — Expanded MACE is a planned secondary objective but is described as post hoc in the report

- **Exact locations and roles:** PLAN [protocol p.9, section 2.2.3, lines 259–263](../../../joi170166supp1_prod.pdf#page=9) and [SAP pp.2–3, section 2.1.2, lines 1071–1074](../../../joi170166supp2_prod.pdf#page=2); REPORT [main p.3, Outcomes and Statistical Analysis](../../../jama_huffman_2018_oi_170166.pdf#page=3), [main p.8, Secondary and Post Hoc Analyses](../../../jama_huffman_2018_oi_170166.pdf#page=8), [main p.9, result continuation and Discussion](../../../jama_huffman_2018_oi_170166.pdf#page=9), [supplement p.1 contents](../../../joi170166supp3_prod.pdf#page=1), and [supplement p.23 eTable 7 title, expanded-composite row and footnotes](../../../joi170166supp3_prod.pdf#page=23). The SAP statement continues on [SAP p.3](../../../joi170166supp2_prod.pdf#page=3).
- **Printed statements and values:** The protocol and SAP put intervention effects on in-hospital and 30-day expanded MACE under secondary objectives. The report repeatedly calls the expanded composite analysis post hoc; eTable 7 calls the analyses exploratory. The reported expanded composite adds incident in-hospital heart failure, cardiogenic shock and cardiac arrest to 30-day MACE. Control 919 (9.1%) and intervention 795 (7.0%); cluster-adjusted OR 0.89 (0.80–1.00); primary cluster-and-time-adjusted difference −1.34 percentage points (−2.72 to 0.04) and OR 0.84 (0.70–1.00).
- **Comparison logic:** The 30-day expanded-MACE name, intervention comparison and purpose match the prospective secondary objective, but the report attributes the analysis to post hoc selection. The supplied prospective documents do not enumerate the added components of expanded MACE. That absent definition leaves open whether the reported seven-component composite is the planned expansion or a different later expansion. The status/definition comparison qualifies for independent screening rather than being treated as a generic unreported-detail observation. Categorical status and component specifications do not admit a rounding interval or numerical relative discrepancy. The matched report-to-report numerical values reconcile.
- **Focus area:** D. **Proposed review domain:** `protocol_report`. **Relationship type:** `P_R`. **All relationships:** `P_R`.
- **Qualifying route:** Route A, category 5.
- **Affected result or extracted value:** The prespecified versus post hoc status of the expanded-MACE result, including its adjusted OR 0.84 and risk difference −1.34 percentage points, and whether that result represents the prospective secondary objective.
- **Why context does not resolve it:** Route A category 5 applies to a prespecified outcome whose reported status changes. The plans' broad expanded-MACE phrase and the report's explicit additional components cannot establish whether the plans and report mean one composite or two different composites.
- **Supported alternatives:** Expanded MACE may have been prospectively envisaged but its eventual component definition selected later; the reported result may be an additional composite distinct from the planned secondary outcome. A supplied amendment could resolve the distinction, but none is present. The report's hypothesis-generating caution is preserved and does not identify the planned expansion.
- **Exact human question:** Is the reported MACE-plus-heart-failure/shock/arrest composite the expanded MACE prespecified as a secondary objective, and, if so, why is it described as post hoc; if it is a different composite, what was the planned expanded-MACE definition and where is its result reported?
