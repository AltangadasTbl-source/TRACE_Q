**Reported-versus-comparator:** Intensive telephone: REPORT 90 minutes versus PLAN 20. Shared readiness: REPORT 30 minutes versus PLAN 45.

**Mechanical evidence recheck:** Exact supplied pages confirm all four durations, both modes and shared curriculum; visual PLAN figure reading confirms 45 minutes. Both minimum gaps reproduce. No stable transcription, role or classification change was required.
