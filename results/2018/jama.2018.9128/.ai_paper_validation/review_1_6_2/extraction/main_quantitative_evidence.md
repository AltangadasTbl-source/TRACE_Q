## General quantitative and design relationships

M001 (pp1–3): Randomized population 610 parent-child pairs; intervention 304; control 306. Preschool child age 3–5 years; trial duration 36 months. BMI defined weight kg/height m squared throughout. Key Points p2 repeats 610 and both groups' mean 17.8 at month 36, with no significant trajectory difference. Recruitment August 2012–May 2014; final follow-up October 2015–June 2017; p2 gives 54 physicians' offices/community settings.

M002 (pp1–3): Intervention 12 weekly sessions; p2 specifies 12-week intensive phase, weekly 90-minute skills-building sessions in person or by phone; 9-month maintenance monthly coaching; 24-month sustainability cues (texts, personalized letters, monthly calls). Control six school-readiness sessions across 36 months; p3 specifies six 30-minute group activities concurrent with data collection, received by both arms. Adaptive additional call when BMI category increased or remained obese at a collection time.

M003 (p2): Eligibility child BMI ≥50th and <95th percentile; service qualification at least 1 underserved-population service; residence/work within 8-km center radius. Individual randomization stratified by community center and parent language; computer-generated randomly permuted block sizes 2–6; concealed until enrollment. These definitions locate analysis-unit and plan comparisons, without inference about implementation.

M004 (p3, Outcomes): Prespecified primary child BMI trajectory across 36 months, linear and quadratic terms. Seven named prespecified secondary outcomes: daily kcal; percentage energy fat, carbohydrate, protein; minutes rest/sedentary; minutes MVPA; center use child never/at least once. Child obesity prevalence BMI ≥95th percentile is identified as only post-hoc outcome analyzed. Parent anthropometrics and child waist/triceps collected but not secondary outcomes in this analysis.

M005 (p4): Collection baseline, 3,9,12,24,36 months; height nearest 0.1 cm; weight nearest 0.1 kg. Annual accelerometer 24 h/day for 7 consecutive days; validated activity cut points. Annual 24-hour recall on 2 weekdays and 1 weekend day, NDS-R. Center use 6-point scale never to every day, dichotomized. Food-security 6-item survey. Collection elements encouraged within 45 days of anthropometry.
