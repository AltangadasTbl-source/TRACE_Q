### F-XS-001

**Title:** Original primary and secondary anthropometry outcomes are removed from the reported outcome hierarchy.

**Compared locations and roles:** PLAN [original protocol p.16, lines558–575 and Tables2/3](../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=16); PLAN [revised protocol p.67, lines2571–2576](../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=67); REPORT [main p.3, Outcomes](../../../jama_barkin_2018_oi_180075.pdf#page=3), [abstract p.1](../../../jama_barkin_2018_oi_180075.pdf#page=1), [Table2 p.8](../../../jama_barkin_2018_oi_180075.pdf#page=8).

**Printed statements:** Original Table2 is headed 'Primary Outcomes' and lists child BMI trajectory, 'Body Fat % (Triceps Skin Fold)' and waist circumference, each T1–T6. Original Table3 is headed 'Secondary Outcomes' and lists parent BMI, body fat and waist over time. Revised p.67 specifies BMI as primary and a new seven-variable secondary list after study commencement but before nonbaseline arm unblinding. Main p.3 gives BMI trajectory as the primary outcome and those seven secondary outcomes, then states parent anthropometrics and child waist/triceps 'were also collected but not included as secondary outcomes in this analysis.' No result for the original additional primary child adiposity trajectories is supplied in the main or results supplement.

**Comparison logic:** Item-by-item hierarchy matching shows child skinfold/body-fat and waist go from an expressly primary table to collected measures outside the reported secondary set; all three parental anthropometry outcomes go from expressly secondary to outside the reported secondary set. This is an outcome-status pattern, consolidated across affected measures. It is not inferred merely from the seven revised outcome names. The revised list discloses amendment context and is retained as an alternative; disclosure does not remove a reproduced status change under Route A. Display precision is not applicable to outcome-status statements.

**Focus area:** D. **Proposed review domain:** protocol_report. **Proposed primary relationship:** P_R. **All relationships:** P_R, P_P; original and revised PLAN hierarchy also differ.

**Qualifying route:** Route A, category 5.

**Affected result or extracted value:** The trial's primary and secondary outcome hierarchy and completeness of the child adiposity endpoint results.

**Why context does not resolve it:** Route A category5 always registers a reproduced prespecified outcome-status change; the revised protocol explains the existence of a new secondary list but does not report the originally primary child adiposity results.

**Supported alternatives:** Original Table2 may have used an overly broad operational primary heading for adjunct anthropometry; the seven-variable revised list may formally supersede the earlier hierarchy. Main explicitly limits which collected outcomes enter this analysis. Those source-supported possibilities remain for human adjudication.

**Human question:** What formal outcome status and amendment history apply to child skinfold/body-fat, child waist and parental anthropometry, and where are any originally primary child adiposity analyses reported?
