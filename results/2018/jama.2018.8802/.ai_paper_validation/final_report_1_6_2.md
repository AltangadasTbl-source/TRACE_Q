**Pending Human Adjudication.**

**Candidate statement:** The plan and results supplement define the composite as total fulfilled opportunities divided by total eligible opportunities, while the main report explicitly averages patient-specific proportions. **Review domain:** protocol_report

**Relationship type:** P_R

**All relationships:** P_R, S_C

**Compared document roles:** PLAN protocol composite definition vs REPORT main composite definition; REPORT results supplement composite definition vs REPORT main composite definition

**Evidence state:** reproduced

**Inclusion audit:** CONFIRMED

**Finding IDs:** F-XS-003, F-ST1-002

**Qualifying route:** Route A, category 5.

**Classification rationale:** The prospective protocol is PLAN; the main and results supplement are distinct REPORT documents. Their differing definitions establish P_R and S_C, with P_R taking precedence. This is a primary composite-definition issue in protocol_report. Opportunity-level modeling passages distinguish estimands and do not add another conflicting relationship.

**Classification ambiguity:** Aggregate wording may intend patient averaging, or descriptive and inferential estimands may intentionally differ. No connecting definition or disclosed amendment is supplied.

**Exact source locations:** [Protocol p.16, lines216–220](../joi180070supp1_prod.pdf#page=16); [results supplement p.2, eAppendix](../joi180070supp2_prod.pdf#page=2); [main p.3, Outcomes composite definition](../jama_wang_2018_oi_180070.pdf#page=3); [main p.7, Table 2 composite row](../jama_wang_2018_oi_180070.pdf#page=7). Opportunity-analysis context: [protocol p.18](../joi180070supp1_prod.pdf#page=18), [protocol p.19](../joi180070supp1_prod.pdf#page=19), and [main p.4](../jama_wang_2018_oi_180070.pdf#page=4). The higher-precision result is in the [main abstract p.1](../jama_wang_2018_oi_180070.pdf#page=1).

**Source evidence:** The protocol and eAppendix use total interventions performed over total possible eligible interventions; the eAppendix says endpoint definitions match the randomized phase. Main Outcomes calculates the ratio for each patient and then averages. Eligibility depends on the patient. Table 2 reports composite 88.2 (SD15.1)% versus 84.8 (SD18.2)%, adjusted difference 3.5 (95%CI0.7–6.4) percentage points, P=.02; the abstract gives 3.54 (0.68–6.40).

**Reported-versus-comparator:** Main: equally weighted mean of patient proportions. Plan and supplement: opportunity-weighted aggregate proportion.

**Reasoning procedure:** Match the same primary composite definition across all three documents; express each description algebraically without replacing adjusted model estimates with crude contrasts.

**Calculation:** For patient i, let r_i be fulfilled opportunities, k_i eligible opportunities, and p_i=r_i/k_i. Plan/supplement A=Σr_i/Σk_i=Σk_i p_i/Σk_i; main B=Σp_i/n. Thus A−B=Cov_n(k,p)/mean(k). They can coincide for particular data, but are distinct weighting definitions. No trial-level numerical difference is calculated because individual opportunities are unavailable.

**Affected result or extracted value:** Definition and weighting represented by the primary composite means, SDs and adjusted absolute difference.

**Why context does not resolve it:** Route A category 5 covers a reproduced changed composite definition. The separately described opportunity-level OR model does not identify which weighting generated the descriptive composite or its adjusted difference.

**Alternative source-grounded interpretations:** Aggregate language may abbreviate a patient average; distinct descriptive and inferential estimands may be intentional. The supplied package does not identify the implemented scoring/weighting algorithm or provide patient-level opportunities to connect those descriptions.

**Mechanical evidence recheck:** Fresh direct extraction and visual rendering confirmed all three definition locations, Table 2 values and opportunity-model context. The rechecker and auditor retained P_R/S_C, protocol_report and reproduced. [Canonical recheck](review_1_6_2/verification/evidence_recheck.md) records the exact observations.

**Potential downstream evidence impact:** If confirmed, this could affect the composite estimand/weighting recorded beside the displayed adherence difference in an evidence-extraction dataset. No realized change in that difference is established.
