## Reproduction calculations and limitations

The Table 2 difference check used d=100(a/249−b/252) and CI=d±1.95996398454×100×sqrt[p B(1−p B)/249+p M(1−p M)/252]. The twelve distinct non-NA rows reproduce all printed differences/limits except S010's negative upper endpoint. Counts are inputs rather than modeled HR s. The point itself printed[−.45, −.35] cannot lie inside the interval ending[−1.75, −1.65], so route A category 1 is independent of CI method.

Fisher checks conditioned on group sizes 249/252 and totalrowevents, summing hypergeometric tables with probability no greater than the observed table; exact two-sided variants are not asserted as the source's undocumented SAS option. All fifteen printed R 24 P values are reproduced by that convention; three chi-square-taggedrows also satisfy the display rule with continuity correction. Neither Pearson nor continuity correction is imposed as an unprinted preferred choice. The method change from planned Fisher to reported chi-square is assessed separately from numeric Pcompatibility.
