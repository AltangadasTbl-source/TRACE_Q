## Arithmetic and coverage rules

Exact participant counts are integers. Every count/percentage and sum check also permits half a printed unit for each displayed input and output; this conservative envelope cannot create a qualifying discrepancy. Percentage discrepancy is the gap between permitted input-derived and printed intervals divided by the absolute printed percentage. Every nonzero gap at or below 0.05 is excluded. A count is not allowed to be changed merely to fit a displayed percentage. Bayesian posterior probabilities, inverse-weighted AE estimates and GEE pathway probabilities are distinguished from crude ratios. No OR, RR, HR, incidence-rate or person-time coefficient is displayed as an observed effect; observed effects use probabilities and percentage-point RDs, with NNT in eTable 4.
