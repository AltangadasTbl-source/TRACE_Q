Fitness definitions: USABLE covers its named locations as a locator/transcription aid; PARTIAL omits or corrupts some content or contains legacy scope instructions that must be ignored; DUPLICATE provides overlapping coverage without adding units; STALE cannot be matched to a current supplied source; UNREADABLE cannot be read. PNG files were identified directly with file; no new rendering/OCR was performed in curation.

Engine provenance: existing normalized text is native/layout PDF text as identified by the page manifest and direct content matching; historical engine version is not recorded. No fallback environment or Office extractor is needed.

Commands used for fitness checks: sha256sum -- SOURCE; pdfinfo SOURCE; pdftotext -f N -l N SOURCE -; pdftotext -layout -f N -l N SOURCE -; cmp against process-substitution output; tr -d '[:space:]' for whitespace-neutral text comparison; file IMAGE.
